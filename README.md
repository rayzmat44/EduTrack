# EduTrack

EduTrack is a comprehensive School Management System designed to digitize and streamline academic, administrative, and financial operations for secondary schools. The platform centralizes school data into a single system, enabling efficient management of students, staff, academics, attendance, examinations, fees, and communication.

## Features

### Student Management

* Student registration and profiles
* Student enrollment and class assignment
* Academic history tracking
* Student document management

### Academic Management

* Subject and curriculum management
* Class and stream management
* Timetable scheduling
* Assignment management

### Attendance Management

* Student attendance tracking
* Staff attendance tracking
* Attendance reports and analytics

### Examination Management

* Exam creation and scheduling
* Marks entry and grading
* Report card generation
* Academic performance analysis

### Fee Management

* Fee structure configuration
* Payment recording
* Outstanding balance tracking
* Financial reporting

### Staff Management

* Teacher and staff records
* Department management
* Role and permission management

### Communication

* Announcements and notices
* Parent communication
* School-wide notifications

### Reporting & Analytics

* Student performance reports
* Attendance reports
* Financial reports
* Administrative dashboards

## Technology Stack

* Frontend: React / Next.js
* Backend: Node.js & Express
* Database: PostgreSQL / MySQL
* Authentication: JWT
* API Architecture: RESTful APIs
